Season Schedule

Fall: September–December. Winter: January–April. Games every Sunday, 1–6 PM, excluding holidays & gym closures.

🏐

Competitive Format

We use 6-2 (sometimes 5-1) formation with dedicated Power, Middle, Setter, and Right-side positions.

📍

Location

Gym locations in Guelph announced at the start of each season. Check the schedule tab for court assignments.

🤝

Our Mission

Promote active lifestyles, skill development, and social networking in a friendly, competitive environment.
